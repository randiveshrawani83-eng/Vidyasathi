ABOUT VIDYASATHI

Making scholarship and scheme information easier to discover.

Vidyasathi is a centralized information portal designed to help students discover scholarships and government schemes without searching through many separate sources.

The Problem

Information about scholarships and government schemes is often spread across different websites, notices, circulars and other sources. Students may spend a lot of time searching for eligibility requirements, documents and deadlines.

Our Solution

Vidyasathi organizes this information in one student-friendly platform and provides search, filtering and eligibility-based discovery.

🎓

Designed for students

Simple navigation, clear information and an easy eligibility checker make the platform accessible to students.
